SCRTVWR Play privacy policy

Updated November 7, 2026. Applies to the Google Play edition, package srv.scrtvwr.play.

Files and local data

SCRTVWR Play reads only media folders you select using Android’s folder picker. It displays images, plays videos, and reads images in supported archives. Folder access is read only. Remove a folder from Storage to revoke its saved access.

Your viewing history, bookmarks, server addresses, and preferences stay in the app’s private storage. Saved server passwords are encrypted using Android Keystore. The app does not send your media, history, or server settings to the developer. The Google Play edition contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or push messaging SDKs.

Network access and other apps

When you open a server or enable automatic reconnection, the app contacts that server over HTTPS. The server receives your IP address, requested paths, and any sign-in details configured for that server. A document provider you select may fetch files from its own cloud service. Those providers and servers have their own privacy practices.

“Open with” gives the selected file or URL to the app you choose. That app’s privacy practices apply. The developer does not operate your chosen media servers.

Retention and deletion

Media copies and thumbnails may remain in the app’s cache until you clear it or Android removes it. Use Clear history, remove favorites or servers, and Clear cache to remove those records and copies. Remove folder access to revoke a saved folder grant. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local settings, history, credentials, and caches. These actions do not delete your original media or records held by servers or other apps. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Contact

Contact the developer identified in the SCRTVWR Play listing through its App support section.

This policy covers the Google Play edition. Privately distributed personal builds have different permissions and Firebase services and disclose those differences inside the app.
